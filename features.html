About CropGuard AI

CropGuard AI is an artificial intelligence-based crop health monitoring system developed to assist farmers in identifying crop conditions through image analysis. The platform focuses on wheat and sugarcane crops and provides a simple method for checking plant health.

Traditional crop inspection requires experience and continuous field monitoring. CropGuard AI reduces this difficulty by allowing farmers to upload crop images and receive instant information about possible diseases or healthy conditions.

The system is designed to be farmer-friendly. It does not require advanced technical skills, making AI technology accessible to farmers who want to improve their crop management practices.
